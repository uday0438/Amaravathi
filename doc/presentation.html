⚠️ The Challenge

The Freshman Orientation Gap

Entering university is one of the most exciting yet overwhelming phases in a student's life. Freshers face severe informational bottlenecks that hinder their early academic performance and campus adjustments:

  • ✕
    Overwhelming Rules: Strict 75% attendance policy and Continuous Internal Assessments (CIA) are poorly understood from Day 1.
  • ✕
    Scattered Portals: Academic details, syllabus blueprints, and campus layouts are spread across fragmented systems.
  • ✕
    Social Adjustment Gaps: Settle-in routines, transport logistics, and technical student chapters (Chakravyuha) lack centralized discovery.

Campus Compass 2026

A digitized, gorgeous, single-page orientation engine packed with premium UX elements to align coordinates, remove ambiguities, and empower B.Tech freshers:

  • ✓
    Bento Directional Guide: North (Academics), East (Clubs), South (Campus Life), West (Placements).
  • ✓
    Interactive Simulations: Real-time SGPA calculator and interactive campus maps.
  • ✓
    Survival Kit Checklist: Structured freshman checklists detailing mess timings, transit, and seniors networking.
